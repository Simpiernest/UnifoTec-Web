# UNIFOTEC-WEB Landing Page Implementation

I have completed the implementation of the UNIFOTEC-WEB landing page using Next.js 15 and Tailwind CSS, matching the design images you provided.

## Key Features Implemented

- **Navbar**: Responsive navigation with scroll effects and a "Get Started" call-to-action.
- **Hero Section**: Exact matching of the "Technology That Connects Businesses to the Digital World" headline, subtext, and device mockups (Laptop, Tablet, Phone).
- **Features Bar**: Horizontal bar showcasing core service areas with icons.
- **Our Services**: A grid of 8 detailed service cards with custom icons and hover effects.
- **Why Choose Us**: A high-impact dark section highlighting business benefits.
- **About Us**: Detailed company information with success statistics (7 Team Members, 100+ Projects).
- **Our Team**: Profiles for the entire 7-member team including roles and specializations.
- **Work Process**: A 7-step timeline from Consultation to Support.
- **Industry Sectors**: A showcase of industries served (Healthcare, Finance, Education, etc.).
- **Client Testimonials**: Verified feedback from business owners and directors.
- **Blog / News**: Latest insights grid with tags and dates.
- **CTA Banner**: A prominent "Let's Build Your Solution" section.
- **Footer**: Comprehensive site links, contact information, and social media integration.

## Technical Details

- **Framework**: Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS for responsive, utility-first design.
- **Icons**: Lucide React for consistent, high-quality iconography.
- **Typography**: Inter (Google Fonts) for a modern, professional look.
- **Colors**:
  - Primary: `#005FFF`
  - Dark: `#1E293B`
  - Accent: `#10B981`
  - Light: `#F8FAFC`
  - Grey: `#64748B`
